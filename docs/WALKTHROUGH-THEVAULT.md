# The Vault — Walkthrough (spoilers)

Four stations in a ring: **Cracker → Wire → Face → Wheel → Cracker**. Your own
lock code is on the screen of the crewmate **before** you; your screen shows the
code for the crewmate **after** you. Read yours aloud, set yours to what they
read you.

## Per-scene lock codes

| Scene | Cracker | Wire | Face | Wheel |
|-------|---------|------|------|-------|
| 1 · Getting In | **317** | **528** | **946** | **175** |
| 2 · Killing the Alarms | **482** | **209** | **651** | **730** |
| 3 · The Manager's Office | **863** | **194** | **507** | **628** |
| 4 · The Antechamber | **350** | **712** | **489** | **205** |

(On your screen you'll see the *next* station's code — e.g. the Cracker's screen
shows the Wire's code. The Wheel's screen shows the Cracker's code, closing the
ring.)

## Tumbler-chips (the meta)

Pocket the brass chip in your scene when it appears:

| Chip # | Digit | Found by | In scene |
|--------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 4 | Cracker | 1 |
| 2 | 7 | Wire | 1 |
| 3 | 1 | Face | 2 |
| 4 | 9 | Wheel | 2 |
| 5 | 3 | Cracker | 3 |
| 6 | 6 | Wire | 3 |

## Scene 5 — The Vault

Every station sees the six-dial master time-lock. Pool the chips, order 1→6, set
the wheel:

**Master combination: 4 7 1 9 3 6**

Throw the wheel → **Clean Getaway**.
