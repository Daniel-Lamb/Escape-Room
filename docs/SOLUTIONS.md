# Escape Room Series — Puzzle Logic & Solutions

Direct logic and solve steps for every puzzle in all seven games. Full spoilers.
Single-player: Pilgrim's Road, Starfall Station, Wild Court, Gate of Life.
Two-player co-op: Signal Towers, The Looking Glass, Silent Alarm (each lock's answer
sits on the partner's screen).

Each game ends on a meta-puzzle: examined collectibles auto-journal, and the finale
orders them into one word.

## 1. The Pilgrim's Road (medieval, 7 rooms)

**1. Oubliette** — The verse names crows, rats, chains. Count them in the murals: 7 crows, 4 rats, 2 chains. → Set grate rings **VII / IV / II**. Grab the candle stub and bent spoon. (Sun: 7 rays, R)

**2. Guard Room** — Order 5 beasts from four constraints: Boar 1st; Serpent directly after Boar; Stag then Falcon adjacent; Wolf not last. Only solution: → Draw bolts **Boar → Serpent → Wolf → Stag → Falcon**. Then hold the iron crow and click the cabinet back to tear the false panel. Grab iron crow, flint & steel. (Sun: 3 rays, A)

**3. Chapel** — Saints' lily counts: Cuthbert 1, Edmund 2, Agnes 3, Dunstan 4, Brendan 5. Plaque: ring in lily order, shepherd (Cuthbert, carries a crook) first and last. → Bells **C-E-A-D-B-C**. Take silver key, holy oil. (Sun: 4 rays, U)

**4. Scriptorium** — Room is dark: combine flint & steel + candle stub → lit candle → light the sconce. Cipher strip `2:4 · 5:1 · 1:6 · 7:3 · 3:2` = "line:word, first letter," indexed into the comet-and-tower book: no→N, Old→O, ravens→R, tunnel→T, hall→H. → Chest dials **NORTH**. Take still-room key, recipe right half, Edmund's confession. (Sun: 6 rays, O)

**5. Still-Room** — Combine the two recipe halves. Brew: Papaver ×3 (3-notch spoon), Valeriana ×1 (bent spoon = 1-notch), Mel ×2 (2-notch); stir **widdershins** = against the dial's engraved arrow, exactly 3 times; light hearth; bellows to the first whistle; draw off. Never touch the Cicuta (hemlock = fail). Combine draught + meat shank → drugged meat. (Sun: 8 rays, A)

**6. Great Hall** — Drugged meat on the mastiff (sleeps); holy oil frees the rusted wheel. Levers: A turns rings 1+2, B turns 2+3, C turns 3+4; rings need 2,3,3,2 quarter-turns. → **A ×2, B ×1, C ×2** (any order). Take crank handle. (Sun: 5 rays, R)

**7. Gatehouse (meta)** — Ignore the great windlass (signposted trap). Sort the 6 suns by ray count ascending: 3A, 4U, 5R, 6O, 7R, 8A → dials **AURORA**; north door opens. Then crank handle into the socket, iron crow through the drum spokes as the pawl, crank ×3 → water-gate lifts.

## 2. Starfall Station (sci-fi, 7 decks)

**1. Cryo Bay** — Gauges A=4, B=7, D=2; C is cracked. Manifold law A+B=C+D → C=9. → Thaw-lock **4-7-9-2**. Take magnet stylus. (Shard: 6 peaks, U)

## 3. The Wild Court (jungle, 7 trials)

**1. Sinkhole Nave** — Three gate traces: pug mark with no claw tips = JAGUAR; three round toes = TAPIR; the smooth drag-line (nothing walked there) = BOA. → Sockets **JAGUAR · TAPIR · BOA**. Take machete, survey map. (Token: boa, N)

**2. Ferry Pool** — River-crossing; the coracle carries you + one charge (kit eyes bird, bird eyes figs): → **bird over → back empty → kit over → bird BACK → figs over → back empty → bird over**. Take vine cord. (Token: mantis, P)

**3. Painted Grove** — Easel border puzzle: raze the two cross-passes (Heights–Scar, River–Fern); raise all four ring borders (River–Heights, Heights–Fern, Fern–Scar, Scar–River) so every ground ends with exactly three neighbors. Press the judge-stone. Take ochre pot. (Token: tree-frog, O)

**4. Totem of Teeth** — Machete on the strangler vines (drum III). Reliefs give the food chain: harpy ate ocelot, ocelot ate boa, boa ate tree-frog, tree-frog ate mantis. Read top-down: → **HARPY · OCELOT · BOA · TREE-FROG · MANTIS**. Take amber lens. (Token: tamarin, Y)

**5. Morpho Gallery** — Hold the amber lens on the mosaic (reveals the left wing). Complete the right wing as the left's mirror reflection: top-outer azure teardrop, top-inner gold disc, mid-outer violet oval, mid-inner azure disc, low-outer gold teardrop, low-inner violet disc. Take beeswax; combine beeswax + ochre → oath-seal paste. (Token: harpy, C)

**6. Tithe Hall** — Find the hollow (fake) pod among 6 with the balance scale, 2 weighings: weigh 3 vs 3 → balance means the lie is in the two held back, tilt means it's on the lighter side; then 1 vs 1 of that group → balance means it's the third. Present the lie; keep the hollow pod. (No-reset run: pod VI.) Take Vance's brass compass. (Token: ocelot, A)

**7. Verdict Roots (twist + meta)** — Rite in order: THE LIE = hollow pod on the stone; THE WORD = tokens ordered as the totem eats (harpy C, ocelot A, boa N, tree-frog O, mantis P) with the advocate last (tamarin Y) = **CANOPY**; THE MAP = survey map + oath-seal paste, redraw the valley against Vance's compass edge; THE WAY = walk through the roots. → COURT RULES IN YOUR FAVOR.

## 4. The Gate of Life (Rome/Colosseum, 7 chambers)

## 5. Twin Signal Towers (two-player: West = P1, East = P2)

Each lock's answer is on your partner's screen; read it to them. Each keeper finds 3 of 6 bearing-marks.

**1. Watch Room** — Each hatch code is chalked on the *other* tower's wall. West wall shows "EAST HATCH 528" → West reads it, **East enters 5-2-8**. East wall shows "WEST HATCH 371" → East reads it, **West enters 3-7-1**. (Mark S, bearing 2, West)

**2. Lamp Room** — Rotate your four Fresnel panels to the bearings on the lamp-card your partner is holding. → **West: NE, SE, W, S. East: E, S, NW, N.** (Mark A, bearing 1, East)

**3. Signal Gallery** — You see your ship's flashes; your partner holds the codebook page. West sees flashes 1,2,3,4 → East's page A (1H 2O 3L 4D) → **West enters HOLD**. East sees 2,3,1,4 → West's page B (1R 2T 3U 4N) → **East enters TURN**. (Mark O, bearing 4, West)

**4. Chart Room** — Combine half-charts. West sees fouled channels I, IV; East sees shallow channels II, IV; only **Channel III** is both clear and deep. → Both select **Channel III**. (Mark H, bearing 3, East)

**5. Bell Loft** — Toll count = your two gauges via your partner's rule. West gauges 9 and 4, rule "difference" → **West tolls 5**. East gauges 5 and 3, rule "sum" → **East tolls 8**. (Mark E, bearing 6, West)

**6. Beam Engine** — Set your five valves to your partner's schematic. → **West: OPEN, SHUT, OPEN, OPEN, SHUT. East: SHUT, OPEN, OPEN, SHUT, OPEN.** (Mark R, bearing 5, East)

**7. Lantern Crown (meta)** — Pool all six marks, sort by bearing 1→6: A, S, H, O, R, E. → Both enter **ASHORE**. → SAFE HARBOUR.

The Glass side is the same manor reversed. Each lock's answer is on the partner's screen; each side finds 3 of 6 mirror-shards.

**3. Nursery** — Set your four figures to the animals on your partner's card. → **Waking: Owl, Hare, Wren, Fox. Glass: Wren, Owl, Fox, Hare.** (Shard V, 3, Waking)

**4. Clock Room** — Your clock shows your partner's code backwards; reverse what they read you. → **Waking enters 5271** (Glass clock reads 1725); **Glass enters 8064** (Waking clock reads 4608). (Shard L, 4, Glass)

**5. Portrait Gallery** — Each brass plate holds half the riddle. Waking half "holds a black bird"; Glass half "wears mourning grey." Both → the lady in mourning grey with a raven (choose by the sitter, not the number — the Glass gallery hangs reversed). (Shard I, 5, Waking)

**7. Mirror Hall (meta)** — Pool all six shards, order 1→6 → REVLIS; reverse it → **SILVER**. → Both enter **SILVER**. → THROUGH THE GLASS.
