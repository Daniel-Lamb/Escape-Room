# The Looking Glass — Complete Walkthrough

> ⚠️ **Total spoilers**, both sides. A **two-player co-op**: Waking Side = Player 1 (real
> manor), Glass Side = Player 2 (inside the mirror, reversed). Every lock's answer is on the
> other player's screen — so each "solution" is something you must hear from your partner.
> Ask Gus (the black cat) if stuck; his third hint gives the answer.

Design doc: [DESIGN-LOOKINGGLASS.md](DESIGN-LOOKINGGLASS.md).

## How to run it
Open `looking-glass/` on **two** devices/tabs. One picks **The Waking Side**, the other
**The Glass Side**. Stay on a call. Play the seven rooms together; each side advances when its
own lock is solved.

## The six mirror-shards
Each side finds only three. Pool all six, sort by number 1→6 to read **REVLIS**, then reverse
it to the glass's true name: **SILVER**.

| Number | Letter | Room | Found by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R | Parlour | Waking |
| 2 | E | Library | Glass |
| 3 | V | Nursery | Waking |
| 4 | L | Clock Room | Glass |
| 5 | I | Gallery | Waking |
| 6 | S | Conservatory | Glass |

Waking holds R,V,I; Glass holds E,L,S.

---

## Room 1 — The Parlour (mirror-writing)
Your door-word is scratched **backwards** on your partner's far wall. Read theirs to them.
- **Waking enters CANDLE** (it's mirror-written on the Glass wall).
- **Glass enters HEARTH** (it's mirror-written on the Waking wall).
- Waking takes mirror-shard **R (1)** by the hearth. Both go through.

## Room 2 — The Library (split cipher)
Your lock's marks are decoded only by your partner's diary page.
- **Waking** marks ◆●▲■ → Glass's Page A (◆M ●O ▲T ■H) → **MOTH**.
- **Glass** marks ★✚♦♥ → Waking's Page B (★V ✚E ♦I ♥L) → **VEIL**.
- Glass takes mirror-shard **E (2)**. Both go on.

## Room 3 — The Nursery (alignment)
Set the four figures to the animals on your partner's card.
- **Waking: Owl, Hare, Wren, Fox.**
- **Glass: Wren, Owl, Fox, Hare.**
- Waking takes mirror-shard **V (3)**. Both go on.

## Room 4 — The Clock Room (reversed number)
Your clock shows your partner's code, backwards — reverse what they read you.
- **Waking enters 5271** (the Glass clock shows 1725).
- **Glass enters 8064** (the Waking clock shows 4608).
- Glass takes mirror-shard **L (4)**. Both go on.

## Room 5 — The Portrait Gallery (split riddle)
Each side's brass plate holds half the riddle; the keyed sitter fits both.
- **Waking half:** "holds a black bird." **Glass half:** "wears mourning grey."
- The one sitter who is both = **the lady in mourning grey with a raven**. Both choose that
  portrait (the Glass gallery hangs mirror-reversed, so its position differs — choose by the
  sitter, not the number).
- Waking takes mirror-shard **I (5)**. Both go on.

## Room 6 — The Conservatory (tap pattern)
Set your five taps to the pattern on your partner's plaque.
- **Waking: OPEN, SHUT, OPEN, OPEN, SHUT.**
- **Glass: SHUT, OPEN, OPEN, SHUT, OPEN.**
- Glass takes mirror-shard **S (6)** from the dry pool. Both go on.

## Room 7 — The Mirror Hall (finale)
Read each other your three shards. All six, in order 1→6, read **REVLIS**. The glass keeps its
name reversed — **turn it about: SILVER**. Both enter **SILVER**; the silver goes to water and
the reflection comes home. **THROUGH THE GLASS.**
