# Trial by Jury — Walkthrough (spoilers)

Four witnesses in a ring: **Heron → Gecko → Boar → Parrot → Heron**. The figure
your account is missing is on the screen of the witness **before** you; your
screen holds the figure the witness **after** you needs. Read yours aloud, swear
yours to what they read you.
